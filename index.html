Edge-AI Telemetry & Adaptive Rider Intelligence Platform

An AI telemetry platform that retrofits mid-capacity motorcycles into connected, self-diagnosing vehicles using only a Bluetooth OBD-II bridge and the rider’s smartphone. It replaces hard-coded shift indicators and fixed service intervals with adaptive, on-device intelligence, delivering enterprise-grade telemetry at a fraction of OEM cost. Prototype platform: Triumph Scrambler 400X. Delivered against a multi-year roadmap: data acquisition and signal processing, machine learning and anomaly detection, then edge hardware and rider dashboard.

Solution & Architecture
  • Non-invasive vehicle interface: Bluetooth ELM327 bridge over the Euro 5 / BS6 OBD-II port, streaming CAN-bus parameters (RPM, throttle position, engine load, speed) with no wiring modifications.
  • Multi-stream sensor fusion: merged live ECU telemetry with the smartphone's 6-axis IMU and GPS into a single time-aligned data stream.
  • Edge intelligence: quantized TensorFlow Lite models run on-device for unsupervised rider profiling (K-Means) and supervised shift prediction, producing load-responsive gear-shift cues and live fuel-efficiency scoring with zero cloud latency.
  • Predictive maintenance: condition-based wear tracking and anomaly detection (drive-chain slack, engine knock, consumables) replacing fixed-distance service schedules.
  • Rider safety layer: high-G impact logic to separate potholes from true tip-overs, automated SOS with the last 60 seconds of blackbox telemetry and GPS dispatch, and lean-angle / low-traction alerts.
Engineering Challenges Addressed
  • Vibration and sensor noise → digital signal filtering (MATLAB / Python)
  • Asynchronous wireless latency → edge buffering and quantized inference
  • Thermal and parasitic power load → dynamic polling and auto-sleep
  • OEM telemetry fragmentation → modular diagnostic abstraction layer
Business & Societal Impact
  • Riders: lower fuel spend, reduced clutch and drivetrain wear, smoother riding habits through real-time cues and post-ride scoring.
  • Sustainability: optimized shifting and less engine lugging reduce tailpipe CO₂ and unburned hydrocarbons.
  • B2B mobility: anonymized, high-resolution telemetry for insurance risk modelling and municipal road-hazard detection.
Key Design Decisions & Trade-offs
  • On-device inference over cloud: removes connectivity dependence, cuts latency and keeps ride data private.
  • Smartphone as compute hub: near-zero hardware cost and universal fit; dedicated edge hardware is deferred to the final roadmap phase.
  • Standard OBD-II over OEM integrations: plug-and-play across Euro 5 / BS6 bikes, isolated behind an abstraction layer.
  • Unsupervised profiling first: rider styles are learned without labelled data before supervised shift prediction is layered on.
Key Learnings
  • Building ML on noisy, asynchronous, resource-constrained real-world sensor data, not clean datasets.
  • Designing for the edge: quantization, buffering and power budgets shape the model as much as accuracy does.
  • Translating a technical system into measurable value across fuel, maintenance, safety and insurance.
  • Making a feasibility case: phased roadmap, risk mitigation and grounding in peer-reviewed IEEE research.

ML-Driven Equity Price Prediction & Model Benchmarking: Reliance Industries (NSE)

A financial modeling and prediction dashboard built on three decades of NSE data (1994–2025) to estimate the closing price of Reliance Industries. The workflow covers data cleaning, statistical feature selection, multi-model training and a four-metric evaluation framework, ending in a recommendation on model fit and its limits.

Approach
  • Cleaned and validated a multi-decade daily price series (handled NaN / infinite values).
  • Applied SelectKBest (f_regression) to shortlist the strongest predictors from nine candidates: Open, High, Low, Previous Close, VWAP.
  • Trained and compared Linear Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting on an 80/20 split.
  • Evaluated with R², MAE, RMSE and MAPE.
Findings & Model Risk Awareness
  • Linear Regression delivered the best result: MAE ₹4.78, RMSE ₹8.43. Random Forest (MAE ₹5.33) and Gradient Boosting (MAE ₹7.32) followed.
  • Results reflect a random split; historical accuracy does not guarantee future market performance.
  • Recommended next step: time-based validation and lagged features for a realistic forecasting setup.
Key Design Decisions & Trade-offs
  • Statistical feature selection first: SelectKBest trims redundancy among highly correlated price features.
  • Baseline before complexity: a linear model was benchmarked against ensembles, and the simplest model won.
  • Multi-metric evaluation: R² for fit, MAE and RMSE for rupee error and outlier sensitivity, MAPE for scale-free comparison.
Key Learnings
  • Simple models can beat complex ones when features are strongly linear, so always keep a baseline.
  • Validation design matters in finance: random splits versus time-based validation, and leakage awareness.
  • Reporting results with their limits, the way a decision-maker needs them.
